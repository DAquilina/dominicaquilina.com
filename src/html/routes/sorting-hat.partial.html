The Animatronic Sorting Hate

Making Magic Real

A CLI app developed in Python for a self-contained system driven by a Raspberry Pi; involved custom wiring and sewing a servo-controlled custom machined jaw into a hat enclosure and writing a Python service to ingest sound bytes and synchronise the jaw's movement as they played.

Eventually this page will contain a case study of the process of building and testing the hat, but as of right now it's still under construction. I'm happy to answer any question you may have in the meantime!
